Reelo: Project Blueprint
A social movie diary where your taste is your identity.
Final lab project. React Native, full backend, zero cost.
1. Overview
1.1 The idea
Reelo is a mobile app where people log films they watch, rate and review them, build lists, and follow friends. It connects people through movie taste: the app compares ratings to show how aligned two users are, recommends films using similar users, and helps groups pick one movie to watch together through realtime voting.
1.2 One-line pitch
"A social platform where your movie taste is your identity, and the app uses it to connect you with people, recommendations and group decisions."
1.3 Problem it solves
• Film lovers track what they watch in scattered notes, spreadsheets or memory.
• Existing apps are either pure trackers or generic social networks with no taste-based connection.
• Groups of friends waste time deciding what to watch together.

1.5 What makes it stand out
1.  Watch Together: realtime group voting with streaming and runtime filters.
2.  Taste engine: taste-match percentage and collaborative recommendations.
3.  Yearly Wrapped: shareable stats cards.
4.  Offline-first logging: works in poor signal and syncs later.
5.  Poster-themed UI: each film page takes its colour palette from the poster.
1.6 Constraints
• Everything must run on free tiers, with no paid subscription.
• Built mainly with Claude Code, so the architecture favours well-known, well-documented tools.
• TMDB data is free for non-commercial use and requires visible attribution.

+-------------v--------------------------+
|              Supabase                   |
|  Auth | Postgres + RLS | Realtime       |
|  Storage | Edge Functions | pg_cron     |
+-------------+--------------------------+
              |
       Edge Function proxy
              |
        +-----v-----+
        |  TMDB API |

Key flows
1.  Browse and search: app calls the TMDB proxy edge function, which checks films_cache and only calls TMDB on a miss or stale entry.
2.  Log a film: app inserts into diary_entries. Triggers update counters and write a feed event. If offline, the mutation is queued in MMKV and replayed on reconnect.
3.  Feed: a query joins activity_events with follows, paginated by cursor.
4.  Voting: clients subscribe to group_votes via Realtime, and tallies update live.
5.  Recommendations: pg_cron periodically refreshes similarity tables, and the app reads precomputed results.

5.2 Indexes and search
• Index diary_entries (user_id, watched_on desc), (tmdb_id), follows (following_id).
• Generated tsvector column on films_cache.title and profiles.username with GIN indexes for full-text search.
5.3 Triggers and functions
• After insert, update or delete on diary_entries: refresh film average rating and counts, insert an activity_events row.
• After insert on follows, likes, comments: create a notification.
• get_feed(cursor, limit): paginated feed function.
• compute_taste_match(user_a, user_b): returns score and overlap.
• tally_ranked_choice(group_id): instant-runoff result.

6. Algorithms (viva talking points)
6.1 Taste match percentage
For two users, take films both have rated. Mean-center each user's ratings (subtract their own average) so a harsh rater and a generous rater are comparable. Compute cosine similarity on the centered vectors, then apply a shrinkage factor n / (n + k) where n is the number of shared films and k is about 5, so two users who share only one film do not show 100%. Map the result to 0 to 100%.
6.2 Recommendations
• Content-based: build a vector from genres, director, top cast and decade for films a user rated 4 stars or higher, then rank unseen films by similarity.
• Collaborative (item-item): films frequently rated highly by the same users are similar. Precomputed nightly into film_similarity.
• Hybrid: weighted blend, with a popularity fallback for new users (cold start).
6.3 Ranked-choice voting (instant runoff)
Each member ranks picks. Count first choices, eliminate the pick with the fewest, redistribute those ballots to next choices, and repeat until a pick holds a majority. Ties are broken by total points then earliest added.
6.4 Offline sync
Mutations are written to a persistent queue with a client-generated UUID, which makes retries idempotent. On reconnect they replay in order, and conflicts resolve with last-write-wins on updated_at.
6.5 Feed
Cursor pagination on (created_at, id) over events from followed users, which avoids the slowness and duplicates of offset pagination.

8. Design System
• Look: dark-first, cinema feel, poster-led, with a single accent colour.
• Typography: one display font for titles, one clean sans for body.
• Components: poster card, rating stars (half-star), review card, avatar stack, list tile, stat card, bottom sheet, skeleton loader, empty state, toast.
• Motion: shared-element poster transition, spring animations on rating, swipe cards for voting, haptic feedback on rating and vote.
• States: every screen has loading (skeleton), empty and error states.
• Accessibility: minimum 44 pt touch targets, sufficient contrast, labels on icons, dynamic text sizes.

11. Security and Privacy
• RLS on every table, tested with at least two accounts.
• TMDB key kept server-side in the edge function.
• Input validation on reviews, comments and usernames, with length limits.
• Rate limit commenting and logging in edge functions or with database checks.
• Private diaries are hidden from non-followers, and the feed respects this.
• Account deletion removes the user's rows through cascading deletes.
• Block and report tools for basic moderation.
• Spoiler tags hide text until tapped.

13. Demo Data and Demo Script
Seed script: about 30 fake users with realistic ratings of popular films, a few follow relationships, reviews, lists, and one group mid-vote. Without this, taste match and recommendations look empty.
Five-minute demo flow
1.  Sign up, onboarding, land on a feed that already looks alive (30 s).
2.  Search a film, show the poster-themed page, log it with rating and review (60 s).
3.  Show the profile with heatmap and stats, then Wrapped (45 s).
4.  Open another user, show taste-match percentage and recommendations (45 s).
5.  Watch Together: two phones in one group, add picks, vote live, winner appears (90 s).
6.  Toggle airplane mode, log a film offline, reconnect and show it sync (30 s).
7.  Close with the architecture diagram and the RLS point (30 s).
